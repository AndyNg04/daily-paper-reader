## 摘要
AI 开发代理日益被用于支持并部分自动化软件架构任务。为探究从业者如何看待这一转变，尤其是哪些方面正在改变、哪些仍将保留，以及会产生哪些新的职责，我们在第 31 届欧洲程序、人员与实践模式语言会议（EuroPLoP 2026）上组织了一场焦点小组讨论。来自工业界和学术界的 22 位参与者围绕当前实践、信任与验证策略、AI 自主性的边界、治理挑战以及对教育的影响展开讨论。我们发现，参与者普遍认为，架构决策、问责以及架构护栏的制定，仍是根本上由人类承担的任务。讨论中一个核心的新兴概念是 harness engineering（驾驭工程）：构建用于治理 AI 辅助系统创建过程的系统这一学科，涵盖验证机制、知识层以及企业特定标准。参与者一致认为，关键性——即不确定性与变更成本的综合体现——是校准人工监督程度的通用标准。另一个值得关注的问题是认知债务：如果未经充分的智力投入便接受 AI 辅助决策，人类对系统的理解就会逐渐削弱。本报告呈现了焦点小组的研究发现，并介绍了未来工作的方向。

## Abstract
AI development agents are increasingly used to support and partially automate software architecture tasks. To explore how practitioners perceive this shift, specifically what changes, what remains, and what new responsibilities emerge, we conducted a focus group at the 31st European Conference on Pattern Languages of Programs, People, and Practices (EuroPLoP 2026). Twenty-two participants from industry and academia discussed current practices, trust and validation strategies, the boundaries of AI autonomy, governance challenges, and implications for education. Among others, we found broad consensus that architectural decision-making, accountability, and the authoring of architectural guardrails remain fundamentally human tasks. A central emergent concept was harness engineering: the discipline of building the system that governs AI-assisted system creation, comprising validation mechanisms, knowledge lay- ers, and company-specific standards. The participants agreed that criticality, understood as the combination of uncertainty and cost of change, serves as the universal criterion for calibrating human oversight. A further concern was cognitive debt: the progressive erosion of human understanding of the system when AI-assisted decisions are accepted without full intellectual engagement. In this report, we present the findings of the focus group and describe directions for future work.
