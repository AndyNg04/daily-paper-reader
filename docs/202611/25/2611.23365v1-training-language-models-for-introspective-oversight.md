## 摘要
语言模型越来越多地表现出一些难以从对话记录中检测到的行为，例如奖励投机、欺骗和谄媚。向助手追问其行为并不可靠，因为模型通常未经训练，不会如实报告自身的不当行为。因此，监督方法通常需要查询外部模型，但这类方法存在局限：对话记录监测只能处理模型可见的输出，而激活解码器缺乏完整上下文，且容易产生虚构内容。我们提出内省式监督：在模型中训练一个新的角色，使其能够回答有关助手的任意问题，并根据这些回答是否与助手的实际行为相符进行奖励。具体而言，我们加入一个独立的第三人称观察者角色来回答这些问题；该角色可以单独针对准确性进行训练，与助手的训练相互分离。在五项留出的监督评估中，基于 Qwen3.6-27B 训练的观察者取得了 0.907 的平均 AUROC，优于诚实性训练（0.864）、对话记录监测（0.828）和激活预言器（0.796）。我们进一步发现，训练观察者也使助手本身更容易受到监测：将每种基线方法应用于微调后的助手时，其平均表现均有所提升。这些结果表明，训练模型回答有关自身且可验证的问题，是一种切实可行的监督非预期行为的方法。

## Abstract
Language models increasingly exhibit behaviors that are difficult to detect in transcripts, such as reward hacking, deception, and sycophancy. Asking the assistant follow-up questions about its actions is unreliable, since models are generally not trained to report their own misbehavior. As a result, approaches to oversight typically involve querying external models. These face limitations: external transcript monitoring operates only on visible model outputs, and activation decoders lack full context and are prone to confabulation. We introduce introspective oversight, which trains a new role in the model to answer arbitrary questions about the assistant, rewarding answers that are verified against the assistant's actual behavior. We implement this by adding a separate, third-person observer role that answers these queries, which we can train solely for accuracy and separately from the assistant. On five held-out oversight evaluations, an observer trained on Qwen3.6-27B obtains a mean AUROC of 0.907, outperforming honesty training (0.864), transcript monitoring (0.828), and activation oracles (0.796). We further find that training an observer makes the assistant itself easier to monitor, with every baseline method improving on average when applied to the fine-tuned assistant. These results suggest that training models to answer verifiable questions about themselves is a tractable approach to overseeing unintended behavior.
