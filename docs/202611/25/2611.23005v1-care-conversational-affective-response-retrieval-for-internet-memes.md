## 摘要
对话式迷因检索支持在线交流中的响应选择，其相关性取决于语境含义和情感表达。我们使用来自豆瓣和 Imgflip 的两个语言特定数据集研究这一任务，数据集共包含 98,506 条迷因记录，涉及 5,583 个讨论主题。帖子串归属提供弱相关性标签，点赞则提供隐式排序反馈。自动提取的情感、面部表情指标和跨模态一致性特征，为参与度探索性分析提供支持。我们提出 CARE（对话式情感响应检索）框架，将情感增强型 CLIP 检索器与基于 Jina 的重排序器相结合。检索器通过门控融合引入自由形式的情感描述，并利用每个查询对应的多个正例进行学习。重排序器结合查询与图像之间的交互及情感表征，并使用基于参与度的排序监督进行训练。在所报告的主题留出评估方案下，CARE 在豆瓣和 Imgflip 上的 Hit@10 分别达到 24.03% 和 49.33%；相比之下，微调后的 CLIP 分别为 23.16% 和 41.07%。这些结果支持完整的两阶段系统在找回与帖子串相关联的迷因方面的效用，但并不能独立证明其感知到的社交共鸣，也不能分离情感因素与重排序器训练各自的贡献。本研究为进一步评估情感感知检索奠定了基础，后续评估可采用人工相关性判断和受控比较。

## Abstract
Conversational meme retrieval supports response selection in online communication, where relevance depends on both contextual meaning and affective expression. We study this task using two language-specific collections from Douban and Imgflip, containing 98,506 meme records across 5,583 discussion topics. Thread membership supplies weak relevance labels, while likes provide implicit ranking feedback. Automatically extracted sentiment, facial-expression indicators, and cross-modal consistency features support an exploratory analysis of engagement. We propose CARE, a Conversational Affective Response Retrieval framework that combines an emotion-enhanced CLIP retriever with a Jina-based reranker. The retriever incorporates free-form emotion descriptors through gated fusion and learns from multiple positive examples per query. The reranker combines query--image interactions with emotion representations and is trained using engagement-based ranking supervision. Under the reported topic-held-out protocol, CARE achieves Hit@10 of 24.03\% on Douban and 49.33\% on Imgflip, compared with 23.16\% and 41.07\%, respectively, for fine-tuned CLIP. These results support the utility of the complete two-stage system for recovering thread-associated memes. They do not independently establish perceived social resonance or isolate the contribution of affect from reranker training. The study provides a basis for further evaluation of affect-aware retrieval with human relevance judgments and controlled comparisons.
