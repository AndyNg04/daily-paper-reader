## 摘要
情感启动是一种社会科学中的标准情绪操纵方法：要求参与者书写回忆中的事件，例如曾感到感激或缺乏掌控感的时刻，随后测量由此诱发的状态及其对相关判断的溢出效应。该方法也会改变基于大语言模型的数字孪生的响应。然而，书写任务需要生成完整文本。我们检验激活引导能否取而代之。我们在两个领域（感激、缺乏掌控感）中，使用 Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct 对 100 个 persona 进行研究，比较两种路径：一种是真实提示路径，复现原始 Twin-2K-500 Mega Study（J2）；另一种是注入路径，即跳过书写任务，并以不同强度将从人类书写文本中提取的均值差异方向添加到残差流中。在 6 项比较中的 5 项里，真实提示的效应介于原始人类效应与数字孪生效应之间。注入会以剂量依赖的方式改变操纵检验指标，并在感激领域的某一强度下（β 约为 2.7）匹配真实提示效应；但超过该强度后，注入效应会超出真实提示效应，近端测量指标上也如此，而且在两个方向上的表现并不对称。结构匹配的无注入基线区分了差异的两个来源：省略书写任务会提高两个领域的近端测量指标，而注入方向本身则会改变缺乏掌控感领域的远端测量指标。因此，替代效果取决于测量指标：针对一种状态诱发方式所确立的效度，并不能均匀地迁移到另一种方式。

## Abstract
Affective priming -- asking participants to write about a recalled event, such as a time they felt grateful or lacked control, and then measuring the induced state and its spillover onto related judgments -- is a standard way to manipulate emotion in the social sciences, and it also shifts the responses of LLM-based digital twins. The writing task, however, requires generating a full text. We test whether activation steering can replace it. Using Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct on 100 personas in two domains (gratitude, lack of control), we compare a real-prompt path replicating the original Twin-2K-500 Mega Study (J2) with an injection path that skips the writing and adds a mean-difference direction, extracted from human writing, to the residual stream at varying strengths. The real-prompt effects fall between the original human and digital-twin effects in 5 of 6 comparisons. Injection shifts the manipulation check in a dose-dependent way and matches the real-prompt effect at one strength in the gratitude domain (beta ~ 2.7), but overshoots beyond it, on the proximal measure as well, and asymmetrically across the two directions. A structure-matched No-Injection baseline separates two sources of difference: omitting the writing task raises the proximal measure in both domains, whereas the injected direction itself moves a distal measure in the lack-of-control domain. Substitution is therefore measure-dependent: validity established for one way of inducing a state does not carry over uniformly to another.
