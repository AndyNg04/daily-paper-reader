## 摘要
智能体资源的价值取决于其骨干模型及配套资源，且只有在执行后才能确定。基于令牌的生成器仍受限于其训练目录，而对资源进行独立评分则无法捕捉互补性；复用已有资源包也往往针对的是其他任务。我们提出任务条件化智能体资源组合（Task-Conditioned Agent Resource Composition，AgentComposition），旨在联合选择骨干模型与工具—技能资源包，以最大化端到端执行效用。我们提出基于检索的捆绑感知型智能体生成方法，从相似任务中检索候选资源及过往配置。经过微调的生成器读取资源描述，并通过受约束的束搜索重新组合候选资源，无需重新训练即可纳入新资源。捆绑感知型评论器借助大语言模型（LLM）教师以及作为执行代理的历史成功配置，对完整配置进行排序，并在可获取执行反馈时据此调整。我们评估了参考配置恢复、采用评判奖励和原生奖励的离线执行，以及基于真实执行的在线推荐，并构建了包含 5,060 个已执行配置的 AgentSelect-Exec。在 AgentSelect 的查询中，我们的方法能够在十个候选配置内恢复完整参考配置的比例达到 67.73%，接近最强基线的两倍；在 SkillsBench 上也优于所有基线。通过代理数据训练的评论器将排名最高的智能体执行得分从 7.58 提升至 8.37（满分 10 分）；加入执行反馈后，得分进一步升至 8.78，高于最强基线。仅需四个目标领域查询，生成器便可适配 SkillsBench。在在线场景中，我们推荐的智能体在大语言模型评判和原生验证器两种评估下均优于最强基线。这些结果表明，可以从任务经验中学习并迁移联合资源组合策略。

## Abstract
An agent resource's value depends on its backbone and companion resources and is known only after execution. Token-based generators remain tied to their training catalog, while independent resource scoring misses complementarities and reused bundles target other tasks. We formulate Task-Conditioned Agent Resource Composition (AgentComposition): jointly selecting a backbone model and a tool-skill bundle to maximize end-to-end execution utility. Our Retrieval-Grounded Bundle-Aware Agent Generation retrieves candidate resources and past configurations of similar tasks. A fine-tuned generator reads resource descriptions and recombines candidates through constrained beam search, allowing new resources without retraining. A bundle-aware critic ranks complete configurations using a large language model (LLM) teacher and historically successful configurations as execution proxies, and adapts to execution feedback when available. We evaluate reference recovery, offline execution with judged and native rewards, and online recommendation with real execution, building AgentSelect-Exec with 5,060 executed configurations. Our method recovers the complete reference configuration within ten candidates for 67.73% of AgentSelect queries, nearly twice the strongest baseline, and leads all baselines on SkillsBench. The proxy-trained critic raises the top-ranked agent's execution score from 7.58 to 8.37 out of 10; execution feedback increases it to 8.78, above the strongest baselines. Four target-domain queries suffice to adapt the generator to SkillsBench. Agents recommended online outperform the strongest baselines under both an LLM judge and native verifiers. These results support learning and transferring joint resource composition from task experience.
