## 摘要
语音情感识别（SER）是情感计算中的一项基础任务，可应用于心理健康评估、人机交互和自动化客户支持系统等领域。由于说话风格和声学条件存在差异，且现代深度学习方法的计算成本较高，从语音中准确识别情感仍面临挑战。许多现有系统依赖大型基于 Transformer 的架构，需要大量计算资源，因此不太适用于实时或资源受限的环境。本研究提出 EmoNet，一种用于七类情感语音分类的轻量级深度学习框架。该模型采用由四个模块组成的深度可分离 CNN 编码器，并结合挤压与激励（squeeze-and-excitation）注意力机制和残差连接；同时，模型还结合双向 LSTM 进行时序特征学习，并采用多头注意力池化机制以改进特征表示。研究使用包含 134 个通道的综合声学特征集，并采用音频级扰动和 SpecAugment 等数据增强技术，以提升模型在多种声学条件下的鲁棒性。在由 TESS、RAVDESS 和 SAVEE 组成的合并数据集上进行评估时，EmoNet 以仅 411 万个参数实现了 96.75% 的测试准确率。与复现的基线模型相比，所提出的模型表现更优，同时在性能上可与参数规模显著更大的先进架构相媲美。

## Abstract
Speech Emotion Recognition (SER) is a fundamental task in affective computing that enables applications such as mental health assessment, human-computer interaction, and automated customer support systems. Accurate emotion classification from speech remains challenging due to variations in speaker style, acoustic conditions, and the high computational cost of modern deep learning approaches. Many existing systems depend on large transformer-based architectures that require substantial computational resources, making them less suitable for real-time or resource-constrained environments. This research proposes EmoNet, a lightweight deep learning framework for speech emotion classification across seven emotion categories. The model is built on a four-block depthwise separable CNN encoder with squeeze-and-excitation attention and residual connections, combined with a Bidirectional LSTM for temporal feature learning and a multi-head attention pooling mechanism for improved representation. A comprehensive 134-channel acoustic feature set alongside data augmentation techniques including audio-level perturbations and SpecAugment is employed to improve robustness under diverse acoustic conditions. Evaluated on a combined dataset of TESS, RAVDESS, and SAVEE, EmoNet achieves a test accuracy of 96.75% with only 4.11 million parameters. The proposed model outperforms its reproduced baseline and remains competitive with significantly larger state-of-the-art architectures.
