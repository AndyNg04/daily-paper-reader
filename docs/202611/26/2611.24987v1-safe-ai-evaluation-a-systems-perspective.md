## 摘要
AI 评估可能涉及一种主动、自适应的优化过程，其中 AI 智能体会重新规划、重试、使用工具或委派任务，以实现目标。此过程中可能出现未预料到的能力或不安全行为，而现有控制措施可能不足以防止危害。为观察原本会被抑制的行为，某些安全防护措施也可能被有意放宽，从而带来额外风险。本文探讨了评估中特有的安全考量及其对控制措施部署、观察与干预的影响，并提出四种支持安全 AI 评估的架构模式，包括多元监督者、独立验证器、静默监控和协同关闭。

## Abstract
AI evaluation can involve an active, adaptive optimisation process in which AI agents re-plan, retry, use tools or delegate work to achieve a goal. Unanticipated capabilities or unsafe behaviour may emerge, and existing controls may be inadequate to prevent harm. Selected safeguards may also be deliberately relaxed to observe behaviour they would otherwise suppress, creating additional risks. This article examines the distinctive safety considerations of evaluation and their implications for control placement, observation and intervention, and presents four architectural patterns to support safe AI evaluation, including diverse supervisors, an independent verifier, silent monitoring and coordinated shutdown.
