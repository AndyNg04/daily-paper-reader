## 摘要
在线 A/B 测试是评估面向用户的系统变更（例如新的推荐策略或排序策略）并决定是否部署的标准方法。尽管标准 A/B 测试中的均值差估计量是无偏的，但其方差往往较高。当处理策略与生产策略以相似概率选择相同动作时，由此产生的观测可能增加噪声，却几乎无法提供关于两种策略差异的信息。因此，要检测较小的处理效应，A/B 测试可能需要大量流量和较长时间。现有的方差缩减方法主要是在数据收集方案确定后改进估计量；近期考虑策略重叠的设计则仅优化两种策略之间的单一流量分配比例，因而无法将流量引导至两种策略存在分歧的动作上。本文联合优化动作的抽样方式，以及如何利用由此获得的观测来估计处理效应。我们的贡献有两方面。第一，基于成对离策略评估，我们推导出用于估计两种策略之间平均处理效应的方差最优动作级日志策略；对于任意固定的控制变量，该策略均有闭式解。第二，我们利用历史日志联合优化日志策略与控制变量，以进一步降低方差。在模拟策略 A/B 测试中的实验结果表明，在所有评估设置下，与现有方差缩减方法相比，我们的设计都能更准确地估计平均处理效应，同时通常还能提高统计功效，并降低选中较差策略的频率。因此，我们的设计使实践者能够以显著更少的在线流量，做出可靠的部署决策。

## Abstract
Online A/B testing is the standard approach for evaluating changes to user-facing systems, such as a new recommendation or ranking policy, before deployment. While the difference-in-means estimator of a standard A/B test is unbiased, it often suffers from high variance. When the treatment and production policies choose the same actions with similar probabilities, the resulting observations can add noise while providing little information about the difference between the policies. Consequently, A/B tests can require large amounts of traffic and long durations to detect small treatment effects. Existing variance-reduction methods mainly improve the estimator after the data-collection scheme has been fixed, and recent overlap-aware designs only optimize a single traffic split between the two policies; thereby, they cannot direct traffic to the actions on which the policies disagree.   In this paper, we jointly optimize how actions are sampled and how the resulting observations are used to estimate the treatment effect. Our contributions are two-fold. First, building on pairwise off-policy evaluation, we derive the variance-optimal action-level logging policy for estimating the average treatment effect between two policies, which has a closed-form solution for any fixed control variate. Second, we use historical logs to jointly optimize the logging policy and a control variate to further reduce variance. Our experimental results in simulated policy A/B tests show that our designs estimate the average treatment effect more accurately than existing variance reduction methods on every evaluated setting, while generally improving statistical power and reducing how often the worse policy is selected. As a result, our designs enable practitioners to reach reliable deployment decisions with considerably less online traffic.
