## 摘要
目前，对于依靠辅助打字进行交流的非言语者，尚无客观且不预设立场的方法来追踪其信息作者身份与自主性水平。问题之一在于，打字者自发手势动作中的细微变化，大多发生在打字者和交流调节伙伴双方的意识觉察之外。这些自发动作与打出信息的有意动作同时发生，但目前并未被视为交流系统的一部分。我们提出一种测量方法，在生态效度较高的环境中探测不同水平的认知负荷。随后，我们与一名新兴言语打字者及其交流调节伙伴合作，对其独立打字和辅助打字时的运动随机特征进行实证估计。在不同认知负荷水平下，该测量方法及生物特征指标能够区分两种运动模式：一是在开放式问题的回应中自发打出短语，二是按照指令打出相同短语。与优势侧（打字侧）同时发生的非优势侧身体动作，不仅揭示了打字者的认知努力和自主性水平，也揭示了其创造性思维水平；后者体现在仅于开放式模式中出现的运动前奏中。这种语义作者身份层面的差异，既可通过表征动作波动 Gamma 随机参数流形上的测地路径检测，也可通过三维物理空间轨迹上的测地路径检测。头部和面部的微动作能够捕捉这些非优势侧身体动作所体现的细微差异，从而提供一种个性化的运动指标，用于衡量认知努力、自主性和作者身份，并提供一套全面的测量方法，为结合触觉支持的辅助打字研究开辟新的方向。

## Abstract
There is at present no objective, agnostic method to track levels of message authorship and agency in non-speakers who rely on assisted typing to communicate. Part of the problem is that nuances in spontaneous gestural movements of the typer transpire largely beneath awareness of both the typer and the communication-regulation-partner. Such spontaneous movements co-occur with the intentional ones producing the typed messages but are currently not considered part of the communication system. We introduce an assay that probes various levels of cognitive loads in an ecologically valid environment. Then, in collaboration with an emerging speaker-typer and his communication-regulation-partner, we empirically estimate motoric stochastic signatures during both independent and assisted typing. Under varying levels of cognitive loads, the assay and biometrics distinguish motoric patterns in spontaneous typing of phrases in response to open-ended questions from those in instructed typing of the same phrase. The non-dominant bodily motions, co-occurring with the dominant (typing) side reveal not only levels of cognitive mental effort and agency of the typer but also levels of creative thoughts through motoric preambles present only in open-ended mode. This level of semantic authorship is detectable in both the geodesic paths along the manifold representing the Gamma stochastic parameters of the motions fluctuations and those of the physical 3D spatial trajectories. The head and facial micro-motions capture these non-dominant-body nuances, offering a personalized motoric metric of cognitive effort, agency, authorship and a comprehensive suite of assays to open new research avenues for assisted typing with haptic support.
