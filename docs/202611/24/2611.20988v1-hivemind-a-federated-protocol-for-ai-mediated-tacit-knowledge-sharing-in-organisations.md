## 摘要
本文介绍了 Hivemind，这是一种联邦协议，使 AI 智能体能够代表用户异步交换隐性知识，且无需任何直接的人际互动。个体 AI 记忆系统会捕捉 AI 辅助工作过程中显现的决策、推理和情境知识。Hivemind 构建了一个共享空间，使这些捕捉到的知识能够跨越团队和部门边界，在不同个体之间流动，从而弥补一项结构性缺口：无论是集中式知识库，还是现有 AI 工具，都无法填补这一缺口，即便已有专家查找系统等局部解决方案也是如此。本文评估了集中式与去中心化设计方法，提出了一种以 Nonaka 和 Takeuchi（1995）的社会化、外化、组合与内化（SECI）模型为基础的联邦架构，并采用设计科学研究（DSR）方法论展示了一项概念验证实现。如何衡量联邦式隐性专业知识共享为企业带来的效益，仍有待未来研究。

## Abstract
This paper presents Hivemind, a federated protocol that enables AI agents to exchange tacit knowledge on behalf of their users, asynchronously and without requiring any direct human interaction. Individual AI memory systems capture the decisions, reasoning, and contextual knowledge that surface during AI-assisted work. Hivemind creates the shared space in which this captured knowledge can flow between individuals across team and departmental boundaries, addressing a structural gap that neither centralised knowledge repositories nor existing AI tooling closes, even where partial solutions such as expert-finding systems exist. The paper evaluates centralised versus decentralised design approaches, proposes a federated architecture grounded in Nonaka and Takeuchi's (1995) Socialisation, Externalisation, Combination and Internalisation (SECI) model, and demonstrates a proof-of-concept implementation using Design Science Research (DSR) methodology. Measuring the enterprise benefit of federated tacit expertise sharing remains an open task for future work.
