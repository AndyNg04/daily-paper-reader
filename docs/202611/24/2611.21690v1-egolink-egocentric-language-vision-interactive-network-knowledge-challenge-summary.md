## 摘要
ACM Multimedia 2026 EgoLink 挑战赛旨在评测第一人称感知、社会推理与交互行动相结合的多模态智能。不同于以可见物体和活动为核心的基准，EgoLink 评估模型能否解读情绪与社会线索、解析基于视觉的指代，以及将用户请求转化为经验证的工具辅助行动。挑战赛设有两个赛道：第一人称视频中的社会推理，以及通过对话、工具使用和闭环执行完成服务任务的交互式智能体。两者共同检验感知、推理与决策能否在离线问答和改变状态的交互过程中保持协同。本文重点介绍赛道 1 的多阶段数据处理流程，包括多项选择题（MCQ）生成、思维链生成、自动质量评分以及视频贡献筛选，并总结任务设计、评测指标、规则、排行榜和具有代表性的解决方案。该基准结合客观答案评分与基于过程和结果的评测，可同时分析最终答案的正确性和执行质量。赛道 1 的最佳系统准确率达到 0.9263，赛道 2 的最佳系统联合成功率达到 0.844，微平均准确率达到 0.916。参赛者报告揭示了一项共同原则：优秀系统会通过候选项验证、对选项稳健的推理、情境感知规划、工具规范化以及状态感知纠错，将中间证据明确呈现，再作答或采取行动。这些发现凸显了过程验证和基于证据的推理对于实现可靠的第一人称多模态智能至关重要。

## Abstract
The EgoLink Challenge at ACM Multimedia 2026 benchmarks egocentric multimodal intelligence linking first-person perception, social reasoning, and interactive action. Unlike benchmarks centered on visible objects and activities, EgoLink evaluates whether models can interpret emotional and social cues, resolve visually grounded references, and convert user requests into verified tool-mediated actions. Its two tracks cover social reasoning in egocentric video and interactive agents that complete service tasks through dialogue, tool use, and closed-loop execution. Together, they test whether perception, reasoning, and decision-making remain coupled under both offline question answering and state-changing interaction. We highlight a multi-stage Track 1 data pipeline for multiple-choice question (MCQ) generation, chain-of-thought generation, automatic quality grading, and video-contribution filtering. We summarize the task design, evaluation metrics, rules, leaderboards, and representative solutions. The benchmark combines objective answer scoring with process- and result-based evaluation, enabling analysis of both final correctness and execution quality. The best Track 1 system achieved 0.9263 accuracy, while the best Track 2 system achieved 0.844 joint success and 0.916 micro accuracy. Participant reports reveal a shared principle: strong systems make intermediate evidence explicit through candidate verification, option-robust reasoning, scenario-aware planning, tool canonicalization, and state-aware correction before answering or acting. These findings highlight the importance of process verification and evidence-grounded reasoning for reliable egocentric multimodal intelligence.
