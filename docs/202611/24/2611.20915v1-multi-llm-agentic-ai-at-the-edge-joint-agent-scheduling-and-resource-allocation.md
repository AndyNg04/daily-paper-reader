## 摘要
本文提出一种支持边缘计算的多大语言模型（LLM）智能体 AI 架构，由一个监督智能体 AI 与多个工作智能体 AI 协同在边缘侧执行用户请求。监督智能体 AI 将用户请求拆分为一系列子任务，并通过两种智能体工作流（并行工作流和串行工作流）动态分派给配备 LLM 的工作智能体 AI，同时采用两级重试机制提高执行可靠性。为延长工作智能体 AI 的服务时长，本文联合考虑智能体选择与资源分配，在满足任务执行时延和服务质量（QoS）约束的条件下，构建能耗最小化问题，并提出一种基于交替优化（AO）的资源分配方案。具体而言，对于并行工作流，该方案将原始问题分解为带宽分配、检索选择和智能体选择三个子问题，并分别采用所提出的逐次凸近似（SCA）、拉格朗日松弛（LR）以及 Gale–Shapley（GS）或基于 LLM 的算法迭代求解。对于串行工作流，问题同样分解为服务时间分配、检索选择和智能体选择三个子问题；其中，服务时间分配的最优解通过 Karush–Kuhn–Tucker（KKT）条件求得，另外两个子问题则通过将所提出的算法扩展至串行场景加以解决。仿真结果表明，与其他基准方案相比，所提出的资源分配方案能够降低系统能耗；基于 Qwen 和 Llama 构建的多 LLM 智能体 AI 系统实验则表明，该方案能够提高任务执行成功率。

## Abstract
In this paper, an edge-enabled multi-large language model (LLM) agentic AI architecture is proposed, where one supervisor AI agent and multiple worker AI agents cooperatively execute users' requests at the edge. The supervisor AI agent decouples the user request into a series of sub-tasks and dynamically dispatches them to LLM-equipped worker AI agents via two agentic workflows, i.e., parallel and serial workflows, with a two-level retry mechanism to enhance execution reliability. To prolong the service duration of worker AI agents, the energy minimization problems are formulated by jointly considering AI agent selection and resource allocation, subject to task execution latency and quality of service (QoS) constraints. A resource allocation scheme based on alternating optimization (AO) is proposed. Specifically, for the parallel workflow, the scheme decouples the original problem into bandwidth allocation, retrieval selection and AI agent selection sub-problems, which are iteratively solved by the proposed successive convex approximation (SCA), Lagrangian relaxation (LR), and Gale-Shapley (GS) or LLM-based algorithms, respectively. For the serial workflow, the problem is similarly decoupled into service time allocation, retrieval selection and AI agent selection sub-problems; the optimal service time allocation is obtained via Karush-Kuhn-Tucker (KKT) conditions, while the latter two sub-problems are addressed by extending the proposed algorithms to the serial scenario. Simulation results show that the proposed resource allocation scheme can reduce system energy consumption compared with other benchmarks, while experiments on a multi-LLM agentic AI system based on Qwen and Llama demonstrate improved task execution success rates.
