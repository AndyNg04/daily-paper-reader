## 摘要
拟人化人工智能系统越来越能够记住个人信息、表现出同理心，并被用户视为社交对象。这带来了一类随着用户与系统关系随时间演变而逐渐显现的风险。现有安全防护措施主要针对单轮对话，无法判断一系列看似可接受的互动是否正在累积性地使特定用户走向受害。本文提出个性化轨迹级安全框架，将关系安全视为一个序贯决策问题：系统根据用户消息推断潜在的风险升级状态，而系统的回应又会影响这一状态。在每一轮对话中，筛选步骤首先剔除那些无法在每一种可能的用户模型下为互动保留至少一种安全后续路径的回应策略。对于剩余策略，我们将行动选择表述为多模态轨迹采样，并利用生成流网络按未来演变的合理性、安全性和效用成比例地生成多样化的未来轨迹。随后，系统选择能够保留最大比例安全且有用的后续路径的策略。我们在模拟环境中评估该框架，模拟依据真实人类与聊天机器人互动的统计数据进行校准，回应策略则来源于公开基准。结果表明，考虑轨迹的决策能够在保持互动有益性的同时，显著降低有害状态的出现频率。本研究将拟人化人工智能的安全问题从回应级过滤重新界定为对人机关系未来演变的个性化调控。源代码见 https://github.com/benedettapicano/ANTHROPOMORPHIC_SAFETY_TRAJ。

## Abstract
Anthropomorphic artificial intelligence systems increasingly remember personal details, display empathy, and are engaged with as social counterparts, creating forms of risk that emerge from the evolution of the user-system relationship over time. Existing safeguards largely operate at the level of individual conversational turns and cannot determine whether a sequence of seemingly acceptable interactions is cumulatively moving a particular user toward harm. This paper introduces personalized trajectory-level safety, a framework that treats relational safety as a sequential decision problem over a latent escalation state inferred from the user's messages and influenced by the system's responses. At each turn, a screening step first discards any response strategy that does not preserve at least one safe continuation of the interaction under every plausible model of the user. Among the remaining strategies, we formulate action selection as multimodal trajectory sampling, and use a Generative Flow Network to generate diverse future evolutions in proportion to their plausibility, safety, and utility. The system then selects the strategy that preserves the largest fraction of safe and useful continuations. We evaluate the framework in simulation, calibrated on statistics reported for real human-chatbot interactions, and using response strategies derived from public benchmarks. Results show that trajectory-aware decision making substantially reduces the frequency of harmful states while keeping helpful interaction. This work reframes safety for anthropomorphic AI from response-level filtering to personalized control over the future evolution of human-AI relationships. The source code is available at https://github.com/benedettapicano/ANTHROPOMORPHIC_SAFETY_TRAJ.
