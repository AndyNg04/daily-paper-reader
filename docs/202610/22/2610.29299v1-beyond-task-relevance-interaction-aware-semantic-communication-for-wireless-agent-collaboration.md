## 摘要
多智能体系统中的语义通信通常假设，任务相关信息一旦送达，就会有益于团队。然而，语义信息共享可能改变接收方的决策，并增强智能体间的依赖，从而降低团队效用。受此现象启发，我们提出一种面向异构智能体无线协作的交互感知框架。我们从解析角度建立通信引致的依赖与团队最优效用期望之间的关系，并定义候选消息的团队级语义价值。我们考虑不同抽象层级的语义单元，并构建一个受时限和功率约束的问题，以最大化预测的团队级净收益。我们推导出一种交互感知策略，用于选择至多一个语义单元及其发射功率，或选择不发送。针对两项由验证器评分的优化任务开展的实验表明，完整解答交换会引发更强的结构趋同，并对团队层面的效果产生更不利的影响；相比之下，方法提示表现出显著的方向性。在所评估的各类功率预算下，我们的策略在能耗低于语义感知基线和完整解答基线的同时，实现了最高的团队级净收益。

## Abstract
Semantic communication in multi-agent systems often assumes that task-relevant information benefits the team once delivered. Yet semantic sharing can alter the receiver's decision and increase inter-agent dependence, potentially reducing team utility. Motivated by this phenomenon, we develop an interaction-aware framework for wireless collaboration between heterogeneous agents. We analytically relate communication-induced dependence to expected best-of-team utility and define a team-level semantic value for candidate messages. We consider semantic units at different abstraction levels and formulate a deadline- and power-constrained problem that maximizes predicted team-level net surplus. We derive an interaction-aware policy that selects at most one unit and its transmit power, or chooses not to transmit. Experiments on two verifier-scored optimization tasks show that full-solution exchange induces stronger structural convergence and more adverse team-level effects, whereas method hints exhibit pronounced directionality. Across the evaluated power budgets, our policy achieves the highest net team-level surplus while consuming less energy than semantic-aware and full-solution baselines.
