## 摘要
在线社交网络（OSN）将信息扩散与观点形成紧密耦合，但现有大多数模型将这两个过程分开处理。本文以级联上的 Friedkin-Johnsen（FJC）模型为基础，该模型将观点更新纳入随机信息级联之中，并进一步推进其解析刻画与系统性验证。我们推导出精确的条件分层递推式，揭示扩散实现与观点演化之间的统计耦合，并表明无法得到关于无条件期望的闭式递推式。为克服这一局限，我们提出均场解耦近似（MFA），从而得到对期望观点轨迹的易处理线性递推式。我们还开发了可扩展的基于树的约简方法，在保留扩散结构特性的同时大幅降低计算复杂度，并通过大量仿真验证解析递推式及其近似。利用初始观点配置受控的随机块模型网络，我们发现，与通常导致全局同质化的经典 Friedkin-Johnsen 模型不同，FJC 动态能够产生更丰富的演化状态，包括有限同化与持续极化。总体而言，本研究衔接了扩散建模与影响力建模，为理解级联驱动的交互如何重塑结构化社交网络中的集体观点格局奠定了理论基础。

## Abstract
Online social networks (OSNs) tightly couple information diffusion and opinion formation, yet most existing models treat these processes separately. Building on the Friedkin-Johnsen on Cascades (FJC) model, which integrates opinion updating within stochastic information cascades, we advance its analytical characterization and systematic validation. We derive an exact conditional layer-wise recursion that reveals the statistical coupling between diffusion realizations and opinion evolution, showing that a closed-form recursion in unconditional expectations is not available. To overcome this limitation, we introduce a mean-field decoupling approximation (MFA) that yields a tractable linear recursion for the expected opinion trajectory. We also develop scalable tree-based reductions that drastically reduce computational complexity while preserving structural diffusion properties, and validate the analytical recursion and its approximations through extensive simulations. Using Stochastic Block Model networks with controlled initial opinion configurations, we show that, unlike the classical Friedkin-Johnsen model, which typically leads to global homogenization, the FJC dynamics generate richer regimes of bounded assimilation and sustained polarization. Overall, this work bridges diffusion and influence modeling, providing a theoretical foundation for understanding how cascade-driven interactions reshape collective opinion landscapes in structured social networks.
