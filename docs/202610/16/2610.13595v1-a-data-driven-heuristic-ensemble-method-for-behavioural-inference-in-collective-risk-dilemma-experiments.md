## 摘要
集体风险困境已成为研究合作行为的典型范式，广泛应用于各类社会实验。这些实验产生的数据集日益丰富，记录了个体在连续决策过程中的行动。除了对这类经验数据集进行纯描述性分析之外，还需要一种方法框架来解释和再现观测到的动态，而这些动态可能受到外生偏差的影响。本文提出一种数据驱动的非参数方法，用于模拟个体决策，并提出一套验证方案，以评估所生成结果的保真度。我们的方法学贡献基于经验启发式集成：将个体表示为有限集合的行为启发式，每种启发式均为依据博弈状态中不同可观测特征而设定的条件概率，并采用硬投票仲裁机制，确定个体每轮的最终决策。这一可复现的生成框架可用于不同集体风险困境设置中的行为推断：行为启发式直接从经验数据中提取条件概率，不作先验假设；每轮通过多数投票并以均匀随机方式打破平局，在相互竞争的启发式之间作出选择，且不引入额外自由参数；验证采用模式导向的方法，将合成数据与经验数据的宏观指标进行比较，并以单一启发式和均匀随机基线作为参照；生成的合成数据可用于探索尚未通过实证实现的情景或实验设置。作为一个示例，我们将该方法应用于在巴塞罗那开展的 xAire 公民科学集体风险困境实验。

## Abstract
The collective-risk dilemma has become a paradigm for studying cooperation in a wide variety of social experiments, initiatives that generate increasingly rich datasets describing individual actions over successive decision-making processes. Beyond the purely descriptive analysis of such empirical datasets, a methodological framework is required to interpret and reproduce the observed dynamics, a scheme that may be skewed by exogenous biases. Here, we introduce a data-driven, non-parametric method for simulating individual decisions together with a validation protocol for assessing the fidelity level of the outcomes they generate. Our methodological contribution is based on an empirical heuristic ensemble: agents are represented by a finite set of behavioural heuristics, probabilities conditioned on different observable features of the game state, endowed with a hard-voting arbitration that determines their per-round final decisions. This reproducible generative framework allows for behavioural inference in different collective-risk dilemma setups: Behavioural heuristics are seeded directly from empirical data assessing conditional probabilities, without a priori assumptions; a plurality vote with uniform random tie-breaking resolves between competing heuristics each round, adding no free parameters; validation follows a pattern-oriented approach, comparing synthetic and empirical macro-indicators against single-heuristic and uniform-random baselines; synthetic data generated allow exploration of scenarios or experimental setups that have not been empirically realised; as a worked example, we instantiate our methodological approach on the xAire citizen science collective-risk dilemma experiment run in Barcelona.
