## 摘要
激励能够使个体利益与集体目标相一致，但当智能体的偏好未知时，激励设计便与学习过程交织在一起。本文针对多智能体线性—二次动态博弈，提出一种自适应激励设计框架，使系统规划者能够同时学习智能体的私人偏好，并引导其策略行为趋向社会最优。为此，我们首先刻画了能够使社会最优成为唯一开环纳什均衡、同时确保预算精确平衡的仿射激励的完整族。当智能体的偏好未知时，我们提出一种在线算法来估计其偏好参数，并证明在较弱的、依赖于轨迹的激励条件下，估计值具有强一致性，且无需持续激励或独立同分布轨迹。随后，我们将该估计器融入一种主动学习激励机制，该机制结合了确定性等价协调与递减的随机探测。我们证明，随机探测通过智能体的均衡响应及系统动力学产生充分信息，从而能够基于所得闭环轨迹一致地学习偏好。结果表明，诱导轨迹几乎必然收敛至其社会最优对应轨迹；相较于全信息预言机的累积社会成本遗憾，以及累积净预算失衡，均以次线性速度增长。跨界污染博弈的仿真验证了所提框架的有效性。

## Abstract
Incentives can align private interests with collective goals, but their design becomes intertwined with learning when agents' preferences are unknown. This paper develops an adaptive incentive-design framework for multi-agent linear-quadratic dynamic games, where a system planner can simultaneously learn agents' private preferences and steer their strategic behavior towards the social optimum. To this end, we first characterize the complete family of affine incentives that implement the social optimum as the unique open-loop Nash equilibrium while ensuring exact budget balance. When agents' preferences are unknown, we develop an online algorithm to estimate their preference parameters and establish strong consistency of the estimates under a weak trajectory-dependent excitation condition, without requiring persistent excitation or i.i.d. trajectories. We then integrate this estimator into an active-learning incentive mechanism that combines certainty-equivalent coordination with decaying random probing. We prove that the probing generates sufficient information through the agents' equilibrium responses and the system dynamics, ensuring consistent preference learning from the resulting closed-loop trajectories. We show that the induced trajectories converge almost surely to their socially optimal counterparts, while cumulative social-cost regret relative to the full-information oracle and cumulative net-budget imbalance grow sublinearly. Simulations of a transboundary pollution game demonstrate the effectiveness of the proposed framework.
