## 摘要
社交机器人必须在解读多模态人类输入的同时协调语音与动作。我们提出了一种整合语音、视觉和触觉的多模态框架，用于生成符合情境的机器人语音和动作。我们在虚拟现实环境中邀请18名参与者，围绕五种社交互动场景对该框架进行了评估，并将其表现与人工远程操控基线进行了比较。尽管参与者对自主系统的自然度和社交恰当性评分均高于中性水平，但人工远程操控基线在这两项指标上的评分显著更高。行为编码和定性反馈表明，对话时机、对模糊请求进行澄清以及空间协调，是造成这一表现差距的重要因素。这些发现揭示了开发能够进行自然、多模态社交互动的自主机器人的关键挑战。

## Abstract
Social robots must coordinate speech and movement while interpreting multimodal human input. We present a multimodal framework that integrates speech, vision, and touch to generate contextually appropriate robot speech and movement. We evaluated the framework in virtual reality with 18 participants across five social interaction scenarios, comparing its performance against a human-teleoperated baseline. Although participants rated the autonomous system above the neutral midpoint for both naturalness and social appropriateness, the teleoperated baseline received significantly higher ratings on both measures. Behavioral coding and qualitative feedback revealed that conversational timing, clarification of ambiguous requests, and spatial coordination were important factors contributing to this performance gap. These findings highlight key challenges in developing autonomous robots capable of natural, multimodal social interaction.
