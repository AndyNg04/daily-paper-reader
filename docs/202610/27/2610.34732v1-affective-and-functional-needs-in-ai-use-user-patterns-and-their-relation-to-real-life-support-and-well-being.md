## 摘要
对话式人工智能系统日益被用于满足功能和情感需求，但用户对人工智能应用效果的看法各不相同。现有研究往往将这两类需求视为二元类别，并依据用户的单一主导需求对其进行分类。然而，这种做法无法涵盖同时出于情感和功能目的使用人工智能的用户，因此尚不清楚这些需求在现实中的人工智能使用场景中如何相互结合。为捕捉用户参与人工智能互动的多面性，我们基于社会支持理论，招募1,090名参与者开发了人工智能支持测量工具，用于评估用户感知到的四个维度的人工智能支持：情感／自尊支持、社会陪伴、信息支持和工具性支持。本研究：（1）开发了人工智能支持量表，用于测量人机互动中用户感知到的支持类型；（2）提供证据表明，用户可能同时以较高程度出于功能和情感目的使用人工智能；（3）提出了关于用户现实生活中的社会支持和幸福感的探索性指标，从而获得与理论预期相符的描述性结果。总体而言，本研究强调了理解个体在人工智能可供性使用方面差异的重要性，这有助于依据不同的用户采纳模式设计人工智能系统，而非假定所有用户都能从中获得相同的益处。

## Abstract
Conversational AI systems are increasingly used to fulfill both functional and affective needs, yet how users perceive the AI-adoption effects varies. Existing research tends to treat these two needs as binary categories and to classify users by a single dominant need. However, this approach fails to capture users who engage with AI for both affective and functional purposes simultaneously, leaving it unclear how these needs combine in real-world AI use. To capture the multifaceted nature of users' AI engagement, we developed the AI-Support measurement with 1,090 participants, grounded in social support theory, which evaluates four dimensions of perceived AI support: emotional/esteem, social companionship, informational, and instrumental support. This study provided (1) an AI-Support Scale developed to measure the perceived support types from human-AI interaction, (2) evidence that users might adopt AI with a high level of both functional and affective use, and (3) exploratory indicators of users' real-life social support and well-being, enabling descriptive results aligning with theoretical expectations. Overall, this study highlights the importance of understanding individual differences in AI affordance use, enabling AI systems to be designed in alignment with different user adoption profiles rather than assuming uniform benefits across all users.
