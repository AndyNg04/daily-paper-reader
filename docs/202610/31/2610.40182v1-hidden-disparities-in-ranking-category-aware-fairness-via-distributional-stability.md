## 摘要
我们研究推荐系统中的公平排序问题。我们提出一个形式化的概率框架，将排序策略表示为排列上的概率分布，并将用户效用定义为：相对于理想排序，用户与之交互的物品所获得的曝光量。我们考察用户群体间的效用平等，以及其类别感知的细化形式——条件效用平等；后者要求在每个物品类别内均实现效用平等。基于近期将公平性视为分布稳定性的观点，我们证明，这两项准则均等价于：当用户群体的构成发生变化时，效用保持稳定。对排序策略的混合进行优化可转化为二阶锥规划问题，我们还为该问题给出了泛化界。数值研究验证了我们的理论发现。

## Abstract
We study the problem of fair ranking in recommender systems. We provide a formal probabilistic framework in which ranking policies are distributions over permutations, and the utility of a user is the exposure given to the items they interact with, relative to that of an ideal ranking. We consider utility parity across user groups, as well as its category-aware refinement, conditional utility parity, which requires parity within each category of items. Following a recent view of fairness as distributional stability, we show that both criteria are equivalent to the stability of the utility under changes in the composition of user groups. Optimizing over mixtures of ranking policies leads to a second-order cone program, for which we provide generalization bounds. A numerical study validates our theoretical findings.
