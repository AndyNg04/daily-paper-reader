## 摘要
AI 编码代理越来越多地在一套运行框架中工作，其中包括 AGENTS.md 等规则文件、按需调用的技能、运行时钩子和自动化检查。关于运行框架的大多数研究只考察单一时间点或短期基准测试。我报告了一项为期六周的案例研究，研究对象是我个人的记录系统：一个纯 Markdown 仓库，三种 AI 代理工具每天都在同一套共享规则下对其进行编辑。我建立了一份台账，记录了 118 起代理失败事件、相应的修复措施及这些失败是否再次发生。我还使用 47 条已知过时陈述和 20 条植入错误，测试了系统每月一次的 LLM 文档审计。最后，我在相互隔离的 Git 克隆仓库中，分别在应用和未应用修复的情况下重演了五起过往失败。所有结果都来自一个仓库，且每种条件仅运行三次，因此我将其视为探索性结果。文档过时（漂移）是占比最高的失败类别（118 起中有 48 起），不过其中 35 起是在同一天的审计中发现的。单次审计约能发现一半已知漂移（Claude Sonnet 5.5 的平均值为 50%），且至少 93% 的发现是正确的。在 Sonnet 5.5 上，代理完成了五项重演任务中的四项。在这四项中，无论是否应用修复，失败都没有发生。只有计数过时这一失败在未启用检查时再次发生；而启用检查后，所有运行中都阻止了该失败。在较旧的模型上，尽管启用了长破折号 lint 检查，代理仍有一次生成了长破折号，但检查拒绝了这一结果。关于运行时间估算的书面规则并未改变 Sonnet 4.5 高估时长的倾向。这些结果表明，运行框架中的经验教训可能比其原本要修复的失败持续得更久，而漂移问题依然存在，仍需依靠机械化检查。不断演进运行框架的工具会以快于淘汰旧经验的速度积累新经验，因此应定期重新测试规则，并淘汰已过时的规则。

## Abstract
AI coding agents increasingly work inside a harness: rule files such as AGENTS.md, on-demand skills, runtime hooks and automated checks. Most studies of harnesses look at a single point in time or at short benchmark runs. I report a six-week case study of my personal system of record, a plain-markdown repository that three AI agent tools edited daily under one shared rule set. I built a ledger of 118 agent failures, their fixes and whether they came back. I also tested the system's monthly LLM documentation audit against 47 known stale statements and 20 planted errors. Finally, I replayed five past failures in isolated git clones, with and without their fixes. All results come from one repository with three runs per condition, so I report them as exploratory. Stale documentation (drift) was the largest failure class (48 of 118), although 35 of those were found on one audit day. A single audit run found about half of the known drift (Claude Sonnet 5.5 mean: 50%), and at least 93% of its findings were correct. On Sonnet 5.5, the agent finished four of the five replayed tasks. In three of those four, the failure did not happen, with or without its fix. Only the stale-count failure came back without its check, and the check stopped it in every run. On older models, an agent drafted an em-dash once while the em-dash lint check was active, and the check rejected it. A written rule on run-time estimates did not change Sonnet 4.5's overestimates. These results suggest that harness lessons can outlive the failures they were written to fix, while drift persists and still needs mechanical checks. Tools that evolve a harness add lessons faster than they retire them, so they should re-test their rules from time to time and retire the obsolete ones.
