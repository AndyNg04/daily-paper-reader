tldr: 现有人物交互生成方法多面向单个或固定物体，难以处理复杂多物体场景，也常忽略物体之间的接触关系。UniffordInteractor采用先推理、后交互的两阶段框架，通过微调三维多模态大语言模型预测统一可供性，再按根节点、身体、手部到物体的层级自回归生成动作。实验表明，该方法在人与多物体交互生成中优于现有先进方法。其贡献是统一建模人与物体及物体之间的交互关系，支持更真实、长时程的交互生成。

## 摘要
生成合理且动态的人物—物体交互（HOI）已在具身人工智能和沉浸式 AR/VR 领域取得了长足进展。现有 HOI 方法虽然有效探索了人与单个物体或固定物体的交互，但在复杂的多物体交互场景中仍存在局限。同时，以往方法更多关注人物—物体关系中的接触信息，却忽视了不同实体（即物体与物体）之间接触情境的重要性。为解决这些局限，我们提出 UniffordInteractor，这是一种先推理、后交互的框架，利用统一可供性对真实且长时程的人类与多物体交互进行建模。在推理阶段，我们设计了统一可供性推理器（UAR），采用经过微调的 3D 多模态大语言模型，进行以物体为中心的统一可供性预测。在交互阶段，我们提出分层交互生成器（HIG），将交互生成构建为一种分层（例如，从根节点到身体、再到手部和物体）的自回归预测，并以获得的统一可供性为条件。大量实验表明，UniffordInteractor 在人类与多物体交互生成方面优于现有最先进方法。

## Abstract
Deriving plausible and dynamic Human Object Interaction (HOI) has gained tremendous progress in Embodied AI and Immersive AR/VR. While existing HOI methods have effectively explored human interaction with individual or fixed objects, they still show limitations in complex multi-object interaction scenes. Meanwhile, previous approaches focus more on the contact information held by human-object relation, overlooking the significance of the contact situation of various entities (i.e., object-object). To address these limitations, we propose UniffordInteractor, a Reasoning-then-Interacting framework that leverages unified affordance for realistic and long-horizon human and multi-object interaction modeling. At the reasoning stage, we devise a Unified Affordance Reasoner (UAR) that employs a fine-tuned 3D Multimodal Large Language Model for unified object-centric affordance prediction. At the interacting stage, we propose Hierarchical Interaction Generator (HIG), which formulates the interaction generation as a hierarchical (e.g., root to body to hand to objects) autoregressive prediction, conditioned on the obtained unified affordance. Extensive experiments demonstrate that UniffordInteractor surpasses SOTA counterparts in human and multi-object interaction generation.
