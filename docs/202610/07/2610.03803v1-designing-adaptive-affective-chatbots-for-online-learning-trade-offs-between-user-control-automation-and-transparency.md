## 摘要
在线学习者常常面临挫折、孤独感和动机起伏等情绪挑战，这些挑战可能妨碍他们持续投入在线学习。尽管情感聊天机器人在医疗保健和健康促进领域展现出应用前景，但大多数学习支持聊天机器人主要侧重于认知辅助，对学习者的情绪与动机体验关注有限。本研究通过迭代式设计过程，探讨如何为在线学习设计自适应情感聊天机器人支持。通过一项混合方法需求探索研究（n=38），我们发现学习者对情感支持的反应存在显著差异，没有一种策略能得到所有人的一致偏好。因此，我们提出了三种自适应设计方案：用户控制、AI驱动和混合式协同控制，并考察自动化、透明度与用户控制之间的关键权衡。最终评估（n=25）显示，88%的参与者更偏好自适应混合设计，而非非自适应方案，凸显了兼顾透明度、用户控制与交互简洁性的价值。我们讨论了在情绪挑战显著的在线学习情境中设计自适应情感聊天机器人系统的启示。

## Abstract
Online students often face emotional challenges such as frustration, isolation, and fluctuating motivation, which can hinder sustained engagement in online learning. While affective chatbots have shown promise in healthcare and wellness, most learning support chatbots focus primarily on cognitive assistance with limited attention to learners' emotional and motivational experiences. In this work, we investigate how adaptive affective chatbot support can be designed for online learning through an iterative design process.   Through a mixed-methods needfinding study (n=38), we identified substantial variation in how learners respond to affective support, with no single strategy universally preferred. This led us to develop three adaptive design alternatives: user-controlled, AI-driven, and hybrid co-controlled adaptation, and examine key trade-offs between automation, transparency, and user control. A final evaluation (n=25) showed that 88% of participants favored the adaptive hybrid design over a non-adaptive alternative, highlighting the value of balancing transparency, user control, and interaction simplicity. We discuss design implications for adaptive affective chatbot systems in emotionally challenging online learning contexts.
