## 摘要
尽管通过对话语音自动检测认知障碍是一种颇具前景的方法，但在大型真实世界数据集中，由于受试者自行转诊以及临床核实有限，诊断标签往往不可靠，因此检测准确性会下降。本研究探讨了经神经心理学校准后重新标注诊断标签能否提升检测效果。我们分析了来自720名参与者的138小时对话语音，并依据注意力、执行功能和记忆表现，采用外部临床阈值、Gaussian校准及基于常模的z分数校准，对不可靠的诊断标签进行重新校准。我们使用原始标签和重新校准后的标签训练基于Transformer的分类器，并在150名经临床核实的参与者组成的内部数据集，以及Pitt、ADReSS和PROCESS-2数据集上进行外部评估。基于记忆表现和MoCA的z分数校准带来了最大的提升。在内部数据集中，二分类和多分类的$F_1$值分别从$0.70$提升至$0.83$（+$13\%$），以及从$0.54$提升至$0.66$（+$12\%$）。外部验证中，Pitt、ADReSS和PROCESS-2语料库上的指标分别提升了+$4\%、+$5\%和+$9\%。经神经心理学校准后重新标注，能够显著改善基于语音的认知评估；这表明，在痴呆症筛查临床人工智能中，标签质量而非模型架构才是关键瓶颈。

## Abstract
Although automatic detection of cognitive impairment from conversational speech is a promising way, it becomes less accurate, as large real-world datasets often contain unreliable diagnostic labels due to self-referral and limited clinical verification, and here we investigated whether neuropsychologically calibrated diagnostic relabeling improves it. We analysed 138 hours of conversational speech from 720 participants, recalibrating unreliable diagnostic labels using attention, executive function, and memory performance via external clinical thresholds, Gaussian, and normative z-score calibration. Transformer-based classifiers were trained using both original and recalibrated labels and evaluated internally on 150 clinically verified participants and externally on Pitt, ADReSS and PROCESS-2 datasets. Memory- and MoCA-based z-score calibration produced the greatest improvements. Using our internal dataset, $F_1$ increased from $0.70$ to $0.83$ ($+13\%$) and from $0.54$ to $0.66$ ($+12\%$) for binary and multiclass classification, respectively. For external validation, the increases are $+4\%$, $+5\%$ and $+9\%$ on Pitt, ADReSS and PROCESS-2 corpus. Neuropsychologically calibrated relabeling substantially improves speech-based cognitive assessment, highlighting label quality, rather than model architecture, as a critical bottleneck in clinical AI for dementia screening.
