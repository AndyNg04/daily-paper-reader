## 摘要
在此前的研究中，我们利用基于 Ian Wright 提出的资本主义经济模型进行的主体模拟，表明财富分布和收入分布中的不平等在很大程度上由一个关键参数决定：人均平均财富与平均薪资之比，记为 $R$。该参数反映了累积资产相对于劳动收入的重要性。在模型中，$R$ 值越大，不平等程度通常越高，衡量指标为基尼系数。本研究探讨现实经济中是否也存在这种关系。我们利用世界不平等数据库（WID）和经济合作与发展组织（OECD）的经验数据，估算 $R$，并将其与常用的收入不平等和财富不平等指标进行比较。结果显示，在多种经验分析情境下，$R$ 与不平等程度呈正相关，其中成熟经济体和发达经济体中的相关性最强。这些结果为财富与薪资比作为一种与经济不平等相关的简单宏观指标提供了经验支持，同时也凸显了不同国家和时期在数据定义与测量方面的差异所带来的局限。

## Abstract
In previous studies, using agent-based simulations of a model of a capitalist economy proposed by Ian Wright, we showed that inequality in both wealth and income distributions is largely governed by a single key parameter: the ratio between average wealth per capita and the mean salary, denoted by $R$. This parameter captures the relative importance of accumulated assets compared with labor income. In the model, larger values of $R$ are systematically associated with higher levels of inequality, as measured by the Gini coefficient.   In the present work, we investigate whether this relationship is also observed in real-world economies. Using empirical data from the World Inequality Database (WID) and the Organization for Economic Co-operation and Development (OECD), we construct empirical estimates of $R$ and compare them with standard measures of income and wealth inequality. We find a positive relation between $R$ and inequality across several empirical settings, with the strongest relationships observed for mature and advanced economies. These results provide empirical support for the role of the wealth-to-salary ratio as a simple macroscopic indicator associated with economic inequality, while also highlighting the limitations imposed by differences in data definitions and measurement across countries and periods.
