## 摘要
随机选择的一名社交媒体用户，其朋友平均而言比该用户拥有更多连接；这一网络特性称为友谊悖论。我们利用这一特性监测 2024 年美国大选前后 Twitter/X 上的虚假信息，并比较了随机用户发布的约 2700 万条推文与其转发账号发布的 3100 万条推文；我们将后者称为传感器。传感器分享的新闻质量与随机用户大致相同，因为新闻质量取决于其所链接媒体的政治倾向，而非用户在网络中的位置。经 NewsGuard 评级的新闻链接，无论质量高低，传感器都能以中位数提前 70 分钟触及。在大选观察期内，右倾内容在传感器中的可见度（以每条推文的展示次数衡量）上升约 2.5 倍，而随机用户中并未出现这一变化。因此，基于友谊悖论的抽样无法区分低质量新闻与高质量新闻，但能够及早发现新闻，并追踪平台呈现给用户的内容。

## Abstract
The friends of a randomly chosen social media user are, on average, better connected than the user, a network property known as the friendship paradox. We use it to monitor misinformation on Twitter/X around the 2024 US election, comparing roughly 27 million tweets from random users with 31 million from the accounts they retweet, which we call sensors. Sensors share news of about the same quality as random users, because news quality follows the political orientation of linked outlets and not the user's position in the network. News links rated by NewsGuard reach sensors a median of 70 minutes earlier, across the quality spectrum. Over the election window, right-leaning content rises about 2.5-fold in visibility, measured as impressions per tweet, among sensors but not among random users. Friendship-paradox sampling therefore cannot separate low-quality from high-quality news, but it detects news early and tracks what a platform makes visible.
