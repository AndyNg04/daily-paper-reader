## 摘要
大型语言模型（LLM）日益广泛地应用于多轮交互场景，在这些场景中，模型不仅需要掌握显性事实，还需维持潜在的对话状态。我们将情感状态视为一种隐式状态，它由稀疏的上下文线索推断而来；即使话题发生转变，这些线索仍可能具有相关性。我们探究 LLM 是否会显露内部注意力信号，以便在推理时路由此类证据。先前的注意力干预依赖带标签的域内注意力头配置以及外部指定的目标片段，因而限制了其在不同情感证据分布之间的迁移能力。我们提出自目标内部注意力增强（Self-targeted Internal Attention Boosting，SIAB）方法：通过在合成最小对上进行因果定位（具体而言，采用激活补丁）来定位稀疏的情感相关注意力头，并在答案位置增强这些注意力头原本就关注的内容词元。编辑的注意力头和目标词元均依据模型自身的行为确定，因此同一组注意力头可在不同任务间复用。我们在三个 LLM 和多种提示设置下，使用 ECC 和 GoEmotions 数据集进行评估。在不同设置中，相较于提示匹配的基线，SIAB 在 ECC 上的宏平均 F1 最高提升 10.3 个百分点，在 GoEmotions 上最高提升 5.5 个百分点。与 PASTA 式外部监督结合时，SIAB 的准确率最高提升 13.84 个百分点，宏平均 F1 最高提升 12.51 个百分点。机制分析表明，这些增益源于少量富含情绪信息的注意力头，并显示层间距离会影响增益的可加性。

## Abstract
Large Language Models (LLMs) increasingly operate in multi-turn interactions that require maintaining latent conversational states, not only explicit facts. We study affective state as an implicit state inferred from sparse contextual cues that remain relevant after topic shifts, and ask whether LLMs expose internal attention signals for routing such evidence at inference time. Prior attention interventions rely on labeled in-domain head profiles and externally specified target spans, limiting transfer across affective-evidence distributions. We propose Self-targeted Internal Attention Boosting (SIAB), which localizes sparse affective heads via causal localization (in detail activation patching) on synthetic minimal pairs and boosts, at the answer position, the content tokens these heads already attend to; both edited heads and target tokens are derived from the model's own behavior, enabling the same head set to be reused across tasks.We evaluate on ECC and GoEmotions across three LLMs and multiple prompting settings. Across settings, SIAB improves prompt-matched baselines by up to +10.3 macro-F1 on ECC and +5.5 on GoEmotions. Combined with PASTA-style external supervision, it achieves up to +13.84 accuracy points and +12.51 macro-F1 points, while mechanistic analyses trace the gains to sparse, emotion-enriched heads and show that layer proximity affects additivity.
