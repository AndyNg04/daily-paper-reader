## 摘要
人类的社会感知是渐进式的：我们会在话语尚未结束时推断说话者意图，随着新线索出现而修正解读，并关注塑造社会意义的特定面部、手势或韵律信号。当前的多模态大语言模型基准测试大多针对完整交互片段评估最终答案理解能力，奖励的是事后识别社会意义，而非实时互动所需的动态感知。基于嵌入互动情境的多模态人类交流格式塔原则，我们提出 GesBench：一个以人类为参照、用于评估多模态大语言模型线索级社会感知能力的基准。GesBench 采用累积式话语片段，衡量模型何时形成对说话者意图和社会可供性的稳定解读；并通过由人类注视引导的遮蔽扰动，评估人类关注的区域对模型判断是否具有实际重要性。GesBench 基于脚本化和非脚本化对话数据集中的 6000 多段话语级片段构建，并在具有代表性的子集上纳入了人类对理解过程和注视位置的标注。在多种开放和闭源多模态模型上的实验显示，人类与模型之间存在明显差距：人类能较早形成预测性理解，并更频繁地修正判断；而模型在不同片段中往往表现得含糊，或反复给出相同判断。GesBench 将社会理解评估的关注点，从模型最终能否识别意义，转向意义何时、何处形成。我们的代码已公开发布于 https://anonymous.4open.science/status/GesBench-share-212C。

## Abstract
Human social perception is incremental: we infer intent before an utterance is complete, revise our interpretation as new cues arrive, and attend to specific facial, gestural, or prosodic signals that shape social meaning. Current multimodal LLM benchmarks largely evaluate final-answer understanding over complete episodes, rewarding post-hoc recognition of social meaning rather than the unfolding perception required for real-time interaction. Drawing on interactionally embedded Gestalt principles of multimodal human communication, we introduce GesBench: a human-grounded benchmark for cue-level social perception in multimodal LLMs. GesBench uses cumulative sub-utterance clips to measure when models form stable interpretations of speaker intention and social affordance, and human gaze-guided corruption to evaluate whether regions attended by humans are functionally important for model judgments. Built from more than 6000 utterance-level clips from scripted and unscripted conversational datasets, GesBench includes human annotations of unfolding judgments and gaze on a representative subset. Across open and closed multimodal models, we find a clear human-model gap: humans form predictive understandings early and revise more often, while models often hedge or repeat across different clips. GesBench shifts social evaluation from whether models eventually recognize meaning to when and where that meaning is formed. Our codes are publicly available at https://anonymous.4open.science/status/GesBench-share-212C.
