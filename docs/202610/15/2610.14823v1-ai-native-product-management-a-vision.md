## 摘要
人工智能正日益被用于支持产品管理任务。与此同时，AI 原生的研发（R&D）可能提升软件实现能力。组织实现软件的速度，可能快于其识别、评估并重新审视哪些内容值得实现的速度。本文探讨在这些条件下应如何重新构想产品管理。我们综合了软件产品管理、产品学习、人机协作以及产品管理与研发之间关系等领域的研究，并提出一种愿景：让 AI 成为产品工作中的持续参与者。该愿景涵盖感知、探索、决策、实现和学习。我们提出六项原则，并通过一种以人类专业人员、AI 人格、智能体、技能及共享产品情境为基础的运营模式，使这一愿景具体化。我们进一步指出，AI 原生产品管理与 AI 原生研发能够构成一个持续的产品学习过程。最后，我们提出四个需要通过实证研究加以探讨的 AI 特定研究方向。

## Abstract
Artificial intelligence is increasingly used to support product management tasks. At the same time, AI-native research and development (R&D) may increase software realization capacity. Organizations may realize software faster than they can identify, qualify, and reconsider what should be realized. This paper asks how product management should be re-envisioned under these conditions. We synthesize research on software product management, product learning, human-AI collaboration, and the Product Management and R&D boundary. We propose a vision in which AI becomes a persistent participant in product work. The vision covers sensing, exploration, decision-making, realization, and learning. We propose six principles and make the vision tangible through an operating model grounded in human professionals, AI personas, agents, skills, and a shared product context. We further argue that AI-native Product Management and AI-native R&D can form a continuous product-learning process. Finally, we identify four AI-specific research directions that require empirical investigation.
