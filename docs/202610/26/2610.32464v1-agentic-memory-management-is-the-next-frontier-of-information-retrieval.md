## 摘要
长期以来，信息检索一直致力于帮助人们获取完成任务所需的信息。近年来，随着越来越多的 AI 智能体开始代表用户消费、保留并运用信息，这一理念发生了深刻变化。因此，信息管理如今也以记忆管理的形式发生在智能体“内部”。在本文这篇观点论文中，我们提出，智能体记忆管理（agentic memory management，AMM）研究智能体记忆中信息的结构、分析、组织、存储、搜索、检索与“使用”，是信息检索研究的自然延伸，也是信息检索领域能够作出独特贡献的方向。具体而言，我们首先提出一个 AMM 系统级框架，并介绍一种将记忆对象划分为“常量”（constants）、“函数”（functions）和“算子”（operators）的计算分类法，这些对象各自具有不同的检索语义。随后，我们将 AMM 与成熟的信息检索研究进行比较，指出二者的差异与联系。最后，我们提出一项研究议程，涵盖基准测试、记忆效用的候选原则、结合参数化与非参数化更新的优化流程等内容。我们希望本文能够吸引信息检索领域内外更多人士，从信息检索的视角进一步思考 AMM 问题与研究。

## Abstract
Information retrieval has long been focusing on connecting people with information they need to complete their tasks. Recently, the meaning of this has changed dramatically as more and more AI agents now consume, retain, and act on information on a user's behalf. Consequently, information management now also takes place \emph{inside} the agent in the form of memory management. In this perspective paper, we argue that \emph{agentic memory management} (AMM), which studies the structure, analysis, organization, storage, searching, retrieval, and \emph{use} of information in an intelligent agent's memory, is a natural extension of IR research and an area in which the IR community can make distinctive contributions. Specifically, we first present a system-level framework for AMM and introduce a computational taxonomy of memory objects as \emph{constants}, \emph{functions}, and \emph{operators}, each with distinct retrieval semantics. Then, we compare AMM with established IR research and identify their difference and connections. Last but not least, we outline a research agenda covering benchmarks, candidate principles of memory utility, optimization procedures that mix parametric and non-parametric updates, and more. We hope this paper could attract more people, both inside and outside the IR communities, to think more about AMM problems and research from IR perspectives.
