## 摘要
随着社会代理日益融入人际关系，人们期待它们不仅能提供顺畅的协助，还能应对拒绝、不适、嫉妒、坚持以及其他形式的社会张力。本文将两个研究方向联系起来：人们对代理的注视、靠近和接触所作出的接受与回避反应，以及作为一种模型的情感黏滞性，用以描述负面情绪如何随时间累积、持续、受到抑制并得以表达。我们认为，不应仅将回避视为互动失败，还应将其视作一种关系信号，表明人与代理之间的界限。在这一观点基础上，情感黏滞性为代理设计提供了新的视角：代理可根据社会关系和互动经历，维持、抑制或表达负面状态。我们讨论了若干正在开展的设计案例，包括三方嫉妒、不适情绪抑制、耐心时机以及时空黏着性，并探讨这些案例如何引出关于归责与可问责干预的更广泛问题。我们并非要将代理设计成简单的替罪羊或人造恶棍，而是探究代理如何介入棘手的社会情境，同时使其干预过程可追溯、有限、可质疑且可修复。

## Abstract
As social agents become embedded in human relationships, they are expected not only to provide smooth assistance but also to handle rejection, discomfort, jealousy, persistence, and other forms of social tension. This paper connects two research directions: acceptance and avoidance in response to an agent's gaze, approach, and contact, and affective viscosity as a model of how negative affect accumulates, persists, is suppressed, and is expressed over time. We argue that avoidance should not be treated merely as a failure of interaction, but as a relational signal that indicates boundaries between humans and agents. Building on this view, affective viscosity provides a design perspective for agents that maintain, suppress, or express negative states depending on social relationships and interaction histories. We discuss several ongoing design cases, including triadic jealousy, discomfort suppression, patience timing, and spatiotemporal stickiness, and consider how these cases lead to broader questions of blame and accountable intervention. Rather than designing agents as simple scapegoats or artificial villains, we ask how agents can intermediate in problematic social situations while making their intervention traceable, limited, contestable, and repairable.
