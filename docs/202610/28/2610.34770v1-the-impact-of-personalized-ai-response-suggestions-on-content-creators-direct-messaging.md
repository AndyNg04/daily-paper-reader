## 摘要
社交媒体内容创作者常通过私信与关注者直接互动。他们尝试通过私密交流与关注者建立联系，但往往要面对大量消息。通过对 15 位创作者开展形成性访谈，我们发现，创作者在撰写合适的回复时常感到疲惫，因为他们既要调和受众期待，又要在回复中融入自己的媒体形象。为探究 AI 如何应对这些挑战，我们设计了一套系统，根据创作者的写作风格提供个性化的 AI 生成回复建议，以帮助其保持媒体形象。在一项针对 13 位创作者的对比研究中，我们考察了该系统对回复行为的影响。创作者认可该系统能够缓和可能显得不礼貌的表达，并预期它可以提高回复率，帮助自己维持专业形象。然而，创作者表示，为降低 AI 输出与自身意图不符所带来的风险，他们还需要额外投入精力核验输出内容是否真实可信。研究结果表明，AI 建议虽然能提升效率，却可能使创作者将注意力转向监控 AI，从而削弱其在交流中的投入。

## Abstract
Direct messaging is often employed by social media content creators to directly interact with followers. Those creators attempt to engage with followers in private communication, but they often encounter a large volume of messages. Through formative interviews with 15 creators, we found that creators frequently experience fatigue from writing suitable content, as they moderate audience expectations while concurrently integrating their media personas into responses. To explore how AI can address these challenges, we designed a system providing personalized, AI-generated response suggestions tailored to creators' writing styles to maintain their media personas. In a comparison study with 13 creators that examines how the system affects response behaviors, creators valued the system's ability to soften potential impoliteness and anticipated higher response rates, helping them maintain a professional image. However, creators reported additional labor in verifying the authenticity of AI outputs to mitigate risks caused by AI misalignment. Our results indicate that while AI suggestions enhance efficiency, they can undermine a creator's engagement in communication by shifting their attention toward monitoring AI.
