## 摘要
由大语言模型（LLM）驱动的推荐智能体利用推理与记忆实现个性化，但集中式访问用户信息会引发隐私问题，而彼此隔离的智能体又无法从其他智能体的经验中学习。我们提出联邦推荐智能体（Federated Recommender Agents）这一设定：本地智能体通过自然语言知识协作，同时将交互历史和个人记忆保留在本地。为此，我们提出 FedRecAgent，无需更新 LLM 参数即可实现这种协作。其核心挑战在于确定应整合哪些推理，因为相似的行为情境可能导致不同的选择。FedRecAgent 将本地经验表示为条件—转移—推理（Condition-Transition-Reasoning，C-T-R）单元，并按条件和转移对推理进行分层整合。在这一结构中，系统会归并相关解释，并区分对应不同备选转移的推理。每个客户端维护语义记忆和决策记忆，以捕捉用户偏好及有条件的选择倾向，并据此指导共享推理的检索与选择，从而生成推荐。在三个领域、稠密与稀疏两种设置下开展的实验表明，与联邦学习及基于 LLM 的基线方法相比，该方法提升了推荐性能；消融实验也验证了个性化记忆与结构化推理整合的作用。

## Abstract
LLM-powered recommender agents use reasoning and memory for personalization, but centralized access to user information raises privacy concerns, while isolated agents cannot learn from others' experiences. We formulate Federated Recommender Agents, a setting where local agents collaborate through natural-language knowledge while keeping interaction histories and personal memories local. We propose FedRecAgent, which enables this collaboration without updating LLM parameters. Its central challenge is determining which reasoning to integrate, since similar behavioral contexts can lead to different choices. FedRecAgent represents local experiences as Condition-Transition-Reasoning (C-T-R) units and hierarchically integrates reasoning by condition and transition. Within this structure, it consolidates related explanations and discriminates reasoning across alternative transitions. Each client maintains semantic and decision memories to capture preferences and conditional choice tendencies, guiding the retrieval and selection of shared reasoning for recommendation. Experiments across three domains under dense and sparse settings demonstrate improved recommendation performance over federated and LLM-based baselines, while ablations support the contributions of personalized memories and structured reasoning integration.
