## 摘要
社交媒体用户既与自身社交网络中的用户互动，也接触平台范围内的内容；其中，少数极具人气的用户可能获得不成比例的关注。本文研究社交网络之外的交互以及用户人气如何调节意见簇的形成。我们在一个多层网络上设计了有界信任意见动力学模型，该网络由社交网络与一个完全连接的探索层构成，后者包含潜在的交互对象。我们发现，在探索层进行均匀抽样会增强不同社交邻域之间的混合，降低意见多样性，同时削弱底层社交网络的影响。随后，我们引入一种人气驱动的交互机制，根据随时间演变的人气得分对联系人选择进行偏置，使得被频繁选中的用户更有可能再次被选中。由此，探索层交互集中于少数极具人气的用户，从而减弱探索层的意见同质化效应，提升意见多样性。与单层系统相比，这些变化取决于底层社交网络的结构。随着人气不平等程度上升，少数极具人气的用户可能成为意见吸引子，并在相应的不含人气异质性的模型会收敛至共识的情况下维持意见极化。

## Abstract
Social media users interact with their own social networks but also with platform-wide content, where a small number of highly popular users may receive a disproportionate share of attention. In this paper, we study how those interactions outside the social networks and user popularity regulate the formation of opinion clusters. We design a bounded-confidence opinion dynamics model on a multilayer network consisting of a social network coupled to a fully connected explore layer of potential interaction contacts. We show that uniform sampling on the explore layer increases mixing across social neighbourhoods and reduces opinion diversity while also weakening the influence of the underlying social network. We then introduce a popularity-driven interaction mechanism in which contact selection is biased by a time-evolving popularity score, so that frequently selected users become increasingly likely to be re-selected. The resulting concentration of explore-layer interactions on a small number of highly popular users reduces the homogenising effect of the explore layer, increasing opinion diversity. The resulting changes relative to a single-layer system depend on the underlying social network structure. As the popularity inequality increases, a small number of highly popular users can act as opinion attractors and sustain polarisation in regimes where the corresponding model without popularity heterogeneity converges to consensus.
