## 摘要
间接互惠是解释人类为何会与陌生人合作的一种主要理论：当行为受到观察时，个体通过与值得帮助的人合作来赢得良好声誉。以往研究发现，许多“道德系统”能够通过间接互惠维持合作——每种系统都将一种行为策略与一种评判他人的规范配对——但这些系统为何能够成功，以及这种成功能否推广到捐赠博弈之外，仍不清楚。在此，我们评估了全部 1,048,576 种道德系统；这些系统的规范均以双方的声誉和行为为条件，并涵盖所有双人社会困境。我们发现，所有高度稳定且能促进合作的系统，都将一种“区别对待”策略——即在自身声誉良好时与好人合作、对坏人背叛——与一种“相一致”的规范配对；该规范恰好将遵循此策略行事的人判定为好人。这类系统“言行一致”，既践行其所倡导的，也倡导其所践行的。在错误趋近于零的极限下，只有一种策略与相一致的规范配对后，才能在所有社会困境中保持稳定：即“悔悟型区别对待者”，当自身声誉不佳时会通过合作弥补过失。我们的研究统一了数十年来关于间接互惠的研究，揭示道德一致性是所有稳定且能促进合作的道德系统共有的要素。

## Abstract
A leading explanation for why humans cooperate with strangers is indirect reciprocity: when conduct is observed, individuals earn good reputations by cooperating with the deserving. Previous research has identified many "moral systems" that sustain cooperation through indirect reciprocity - each pairing a strategy for how to act with a norm for how to judge others - yet what makes them succeed, and whether that success generalizes beyond the donation game, has remained unclear. Here we evaluate all 1,048,576 moral systems whose norms condition on both players' reputations and actions, across every two-player social dilemma. We find that every highly stable, cooperative system pairs a "discriminating" strategy - one that cooperates with the good and defects against the bad when in good standing - with a "consistent" norm that deems good exactly those who act as the strategy prescribes. Such systems "practice what they preach" and preach what they practice. In the limit of small errors, only one strategy paired with a consistent norm remains stable across all social dilemmas: the "contrite discriminator", which cooperates to make amends when its standing is bad. Our results unify decades of research on indirect reciprocity, revealing moral consistency as the common ingredient of every stable, cooperative moral system.
