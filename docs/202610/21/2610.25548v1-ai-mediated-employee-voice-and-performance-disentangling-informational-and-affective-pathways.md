## 摘要
员工建言——员工自愿表达旨在改善组织运作的想法、关切和建议——能够为企业创造价值。然而，越来越多的组织开始以AI赋能的系统取代或补充由人主导的倾听，以大规模收集、分析和传递员工意见。算法倾听能否复制人际建言创造价值的机制，以及它是否会改变建言转化为绩效的路径，仍是尚待解答的问题。本研究提出理论观点，认为传统建言兼具两种不同功能：获取知识和传递关系信号；而AI介入可能使这两种功能相互分离：增强信息路径（已实施的改进措施），同时削弱情感路径（员工感受到自己被倾听的体验）。我们拟在一家全球战略咨询公司开展随机现场干预，覆盖约2,000名面向客户的顾问，设置三种倾听条件：不进行结构化建言、由人工主导的问卷式建言，以及AI赋能的对话式建言；并将每周团队调查数据与纵向绩效评定相结合。研究将检验一个双重中介模型，其中建言通过（1）已实施的流程改进和（2）情感幸福感影响绩效。本研究将建言理论拓展至知识密集型专业服务领域，区分建言的微观作用机制，并为AI能否成为有效倾听者提供因果证据。

## Abstract
Employee voice -- the discretionary expression of ideas, concerns, and suggestions intended to improve organizational functioning -- creates value inside firms, yet organizations increasingly replace or augment human-led listening with AI-enabled systems that collect, analyze, and route employee input at scale. Whether algorithmic listening replicates the value-creation mechanisms of human voice, and whether it alters the pathways through which voice translates into performance, remains an open question. This research proposal theorizes that traditional voice bundles two distinct functions, knowledge extraction and relational signaling, and that AI mediation may decouple them: strengthening the informational pathway (implemented improvement measures) while weakening the affective pathway (the experience of being heard). We propose a randomized field intervention at a global strategy consulting firm, covering approximately 2,000 client-facing consultants, that varies the form of listening across three conditions -- no structured voice, human-led survey-based voice, and AI-enabled conversational voice -- and links weekly team survey data to longitudinal performance ratings. A dual-mediation model is tested in which voice affects performance through (1) implemented process improvements and (2) affective well-being. The study extends voice theory to knowledge-intensive professional services, disentangles the micro-mechanisms of voice, and provides causal evidence on whether AI can serve as an effective listener.
