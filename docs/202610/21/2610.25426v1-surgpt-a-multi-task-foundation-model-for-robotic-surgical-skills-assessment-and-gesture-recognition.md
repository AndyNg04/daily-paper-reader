## 摘要
机器人辅助手术培训中的手术技能评估传统上依赖人类专家的主观评价，这不仅造成了外科教育的瓶颈，也限制了其规模化发展。我们提出了一种新型基础模型，可自动分析机器人手术视频，实现全面的技能评估与手势理解。该模型结合视觉编码器与 Transformer 架构，同时执行三项关键任务：1）手术任务分类（打结、针传递、缝合）；2）按照标准化量表评估技能水平；3）以自然语言生成手术手势描述。模型处理来自达芬奇手术机器人的视频序列，并生成易于理解的手术动作文字描述，其方式类似于语言模型根据提示生成文本。模型采用多任务学习框架，并通过自适应不确定性加权优化各项任务的表现。基于 Transformer 的架构引入位置编码以理解时间信息，并利用注意力机制整合时空特征。在达芬奇技能模拟器数据集上的评估表明，该框架在手术任务识别方面达到了最先进的性能，并能提供与专家评价相关联、具有可解释性的技能评估。模型生成手术手势自然语言描述的能力，为自动化外科教育、实时反馈系统，以及外科培训与认证项目中的客观技能评估开辟了新的可能性。

## Abstract
The assessment of surgical skills in robotic surgery training has traditionally relied on subjective evaluations by human experts, creating bottlenecks in surgical education and limiting scalability. We present a novel foundation model that automatically analyzes robotic surgical videos to provide comprehensive skill assessment and gesture understanding. Combining a visual encoder with a transformer architecture, the model performs three key tasks simultaneously: 1) surgical task classification (knot tying, needle passing, suturing), 2) skill level assessment on a standardized scale, and 3) natural language generation of surgical gesture descriptions. The model processes video sequences from the da Vinci surgical robot and generates human-readable text descriptions of surgical actions, similar to how language models generate text from prompts, using a multi-task learning framework with adaptive uncertainty weighting to optimize performance across all objectives. Our transformer-based architecture incorporates positional encoding for temporal understanding and attention mechanisms for spatiotemporal feature integration. Evaluated on the da Vinci Skills Simulator dataset, the proposed framework demonstrates state-of-the-art performance in surgical task recognition and provides interpretable skill assessments that correlate with expert evaluations, with the model's ability to generate natural language descriptions of surgical gestures opening new possibilities for automated surgical education, real-time feedback systems, and objective skill assessment in surgical training and certification programs.
