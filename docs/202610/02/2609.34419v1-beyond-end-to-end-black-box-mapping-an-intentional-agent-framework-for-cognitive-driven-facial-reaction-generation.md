## 摘要
自动生成类人面部反应（FRG）对于构建能够参与人机交互（HCI）的智能系统至关重要。在人际互动中，多样且符合语境的面部反应能够反映潜在的评价与情感过程，但现有大多数 FRG 方法依赖端到端架构，直接将说话者行为映射为倾听者表情，缺乏显式的中间内部状态。我们将 FRG 重新定义为由结构化内部状态过程介导的生成任务，并提出“意向性智能体”（Intentional Agent），通过显式的中间状态，将 FRG 从直接的刺激—反应映射转变为以刺激为基础的生成。为表征内部状态随时间的演变，我们提出一种内部动力学模型，在用于后续生成的结构化中间状态中，将情绪驱动与迭代式“内在思维流”（Inner Thought Flow，ITF）相结合。即使在对话停顿期间，该状态也能够持续更新。此外，为衔接抽象内部状态与生理动作，我们将 FRG 建模为从潜在思维流到面部表情的下游情感映射。在 REACT 2025 数据集上的实验显示，FRDist 为 72.39，FRDiv 为 0.5057；感知合理性则通过独立的盲法人类评分进行评估。对 96 个反应开展的盲法人工评估发现，Full 与真实标注的平均分无显著差异（$5.527$ 对 $5.195$，$p_{\mathrm{Holm}}=.076$）；而 Full 显著优于 Event-Triggered 和 Heuristic-Only（两者均为 $p_{\mathrm{Holm}}<.001$）。反应质量评分器（Reaction Quality Scorer，RQS）与人工判断高度相关（Pearson $r=.855$；Spearman $ρ=.821$，两者均为 $p<.05$），支持其作为自动评价指标的应用。这些结果凸显了内生动力学在构建高度自主、类人智能体方面的巨大潜力。

## Abstract
Automatic human-like facial reaction generation (FRG) is essential for building intelligent systems that can engage in human-computer interaction (HCI). While diverse and context-appropriate facial reactions can reflect latent appraisal and affective processes in human interaction, most existing FRG methods rely on end-to-end architectures that directly map speaker behaviours to listener expressions without an explicit intermediate internal state. We reformulate FRG as generation mediated by a structured internal-state process and propose the \textbf{Intentional Agent}, which shifts FRG from direct stimulus-response mapping to stimulus-grounded generation through explicit intermediate states. To represent temporal internal-state evolution, we propose an internal dynamics model that integrates emotional drives with an iterative Inner Thought Flow (ITF) within a structured intermediate state used for subsequent generation. This state can continue to update during conversational silences. Furthermore, to bridge abstract internal states with physiological actions, we formulate FRG as a downstream affective mapping from this latent thought flow to facial expressions. Experiments on the REACT 2025 dataset show an FRDist of 72.39 and an FRDiv of 0.5057; perceptual plausibility is evaluated separately through blinded human ratings. A blinded human evaluation of 96 reactions found no significant difference in mean score between Full and ground truth ($5.527$ vs.\ $5.195$, $p_{\mathrm{Holm}}=.076$), while Full significantly outperformed Event-Triggered and Heuristic-Only (both $p_{\mathrm{Holm}}<.001$). The Reaction Quality Scorer (RQS) correlated strongly with human judgements (Pearson $r=.855$; Spearman $ρ=.821$, both $p<.05$), supporting its use as an automatic metric. These results underscore the immense potential of endogenous dynamics in building highly autonomous, human-like agents.
