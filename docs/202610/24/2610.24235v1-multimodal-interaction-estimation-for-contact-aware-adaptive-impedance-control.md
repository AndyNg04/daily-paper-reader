## 摘要
随着基于学习的策略不断发展，机器人日益部署于制造车间和家庭等以人为中心的环境中。在这些环境中，机器人既要补偿未知载荷，又要在人机交互过程中保持柔顺，才能实现安全、精准的操作。仅依靠单一传感模态难以可靠地区分不同类型的交互：力信号无法区分负载与人类推推，而视觉则容易受到遮挡影响。为此，我们提出一种与任务无关的交互估计与自适应控制框架，通过基于 Transformer 的分类器融合力历史数据与三维点云，以应对这一挑战。该分类器采用模态丢弃进行训练，以增强对遮挡的鲁棒性，并通过从仿真到硬件的微调实现迁移；其准确率达到 91.89%，且在一种模态完全被遮挡时仍保持 90% 以上的准确率。分类器驱动一个系统辨识框架，有选择地补偿未知载荷，并驱动一个可变阻抗控制器，在自适应模式与响应模式之间切换，同时估计交互力，以供下游任务使用，例如人类意图估计。我们在外部扰动和未知载荷条件下验证了该框架，涵盖单臂操作、与基于模仿学习的策略协同部署、由意图驱动的目标切换，以及在两台 Franka 机械臂上零样本部署并抬起质量未知的箱子等场景。实验结果表明，该框架能够泛化至闭链运动学和未见过的设置，从而实现安全、精准的部署。

## Abstract
With advancements in learning-based policies, robots are increasingly being deployed in human-centric environments, such as manufacturing floors and homes, where safe and precise operation requires compensating for unknown loads while remaining compliant during human interactions. Reliably distinguishing between these interaction types is challenging with a single sensing modality, as force signals cannot separate payloads from human pushes, and vision is susceptible to occlusions. We propose a task-agnostic interaction estimation and adaptive control framework that addresses this challenge by fusing force history and 3D point cloud through a transformer-based classifier. Trained with modality dropout for occlusion resilience and fine-tuned from simulation to hardware, the classifier achieves 91.89% accuracy and maintains above 90% under complete occlusion of one modality. The classifier drives a system identification framework that selectively compensates for unknown payloads and a variable-impedance controller that switches between adaptive and reactive modes, while simultaneously estimating interaction forces for downstream tasks, such as human intent estimation. We validate the framework under external perturbations and unknown loads across single-arm manipulation, deployment with an imitation-learning-based policy, intent-driven target switching, and zero-shot bimanual deployment on two Franka arms lifting a box of unknown mass, demonstrating generalization to closed kinematic chains and unseen setups, enabling safe and precise deployment.
