## 摘要
AI介导的抬头增强现实（AR）用动态适应的界面取代了固定界面，这些界面根据无法事先完全预见的持续变化情境，决定呈现什么信息、以何种形式以及何时呈现。尽管它仍然是一种界面，但其随时间的表现仅在设计时部分指定。我们认为，这种转变需要评估方式的相应改变：从快照到轨迹。固定界面在快照中评估——一个情境、一次会话、一组任务性能指标。流体界面必须在轨迹上评估——一系列带有转换的情境，从界面实际会遇到分布中采样，并跟踪足够长的时间，以便用户信任得以形成、演变，并可能恶化。借鉴光学透视头戴显示器（OST-HMD）实现的抬头AR多任务处理文献，我们发现当前评估实践在很大程度上仍是基于快照的。大多数研究使用固定条件、单会话设计；并发任务之间的干扰很少被直接量化；常用的工作量测量无法解开可归因于单个任务的认知负荷。为解决这些局限，我们主张三个转变：从孤立指标到性能工作特性（POC）干扰边界，从固定条件到情境轨迹上的评估，以及从单会话快照到纵向信任测量。

## Abstract
AI-mediated heads-up augmented reality (AR) replaces fixed interfaces with dynamically adapting ones that decide what information to present, in what form, and when, based on a continually changing context that cannot be fully anticipated beforehand. Although it remains an interface, its behavior over time is only partially specified at design time. We argue that this shift requires a corresponding change in evaluation: from snapshots to trajectories. A fixed interface is evaluated in a snapshot --- one context, one session, one set of task-performance metrics. A fluid interface must be evaluated over a trajectory --- a sequence of contexts with transitions, sampled from the distribution the interface will actually encounter, and tracked long enough for user trust to form, evolve, and potentially deteriorate. Drawing on the literature for heads-up AR multitasking enabled by optical see-through head-mounted displays (OST-HMDs), we find that current evaluation practice remains largely snapshot-based. Most studies use fixed-condition, single-session designs; interference between concurrent tasks is rarely quantified directly; and commonly used workload measures cannot disentangle cognitive load attributable to individual tasks. To address these limitations, we argue for three shifts: from isolated metrics to Performance Operating Characteristic (POC) interference frontiers, from fixed conditions to evaluation over context trajectories, and from single-session snapshots to longitudinal trust measurement.
